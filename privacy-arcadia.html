Privacy Policy — Arcadia

Effective date: July 25, 2026 · Last updated: July 25, 2026

This policy describes how Arcadia ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information. Arcadia is an arcade-style game collection app offering 20+ built-in mini-games for entertainment purposes.

1. Information We Collect

Arcadia does not require account registration to play. We may collect:

  • Device information: device model, OS version, app version, and language settings.
  • Gameplay data: scores, in-game progress, and preferences, typically stored locally on your device.
  • Usage & analytics data: app opens, session length, and feature usage to help us understand which games are popular and improve the experience.
  • Advertising identifiers: if ads are shown in the App, ad network partners may collect device advertising IDs to serve and measure ads.
  • Crash & diagnostic data: crash logs and performance data to help us fix bugs.

2. How We Use Information

  • To run and improve the App's games and features.
  • To save your scores and progress.
  • To analyze usage trends and improve performance and stability.
  • To display and measure advertising, if applicable, through third-party ad networks.

3. Third-Party Services

Arcadia may use trusted third-party services for analytics, crash reporting, and advertising (for example, Firebase Analytics or ad networks such as Google AdMob). These providers may collect information as described in their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review those policies for details on their data practices.

4. In-App Purchases

If Arcadia offers in-app purchases or a premium/ad-free option, purchase transactions are processed securely by the Google Play billing system. We do not collect or store your payment card details.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share limited data with service providers (analytics, advertising, crash reporting) necessary to operate the App, or when required by law.

6. Children's Privacy

Arcadia is a general-audience entertainment app and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the limited data we process. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices

  • You can reset or limit your device's advertising identifier through your device settings.
  • You can disable analytics/ads permissions where supported by your device's OS settings.
  • You can uninstall the App at any time to stop all data collection by Arcadia.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: sportspiritt@gmail.com
